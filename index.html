THE STORY

A warrior searching for what was lost.

Lakan: The Last Warrior follows the journey of Lakan, a Sacred Guardian caught in a conflict involving the balance of the mortal and divine worlds.

At the heart of the story is the Alab ng Diwa — a divine energy that connects the heavens and the earth.

As Lakan travels through different regions, he encounters creatures, characters, challenges, and fragments of a past that gradually reveal the truth behind his journey.

✦

The adventure combines mythology, exploration, combat, and cultural discovery into one interactive experience.
