THE STORY

A forgotten guardian. A world falling out of balance.

Sidapa, the God of Death and Ruler of Fate, seeks to eliminate the Alab ng Diwa — a divine energy passed down from Bathala that connects the heavens and the earth.

During the siege of Tundun, Lakan, a Sacred Guardian, seals the Alab ng Diwa inside his Galang, a sacred bracelet, to prevent Sidapa from obtaining it.

Believing the divine energy has been destroyed, Sidapa leaves Lakan weakened and casts the “Blindfold of the Soul,” erasing his memories and identity.

Players take the role of Lakan and recover scattered Memory Fragments across six story levels while encountering mythological creatures, NPCs, quests, cultural practices, traditional items, and Filipino values.
